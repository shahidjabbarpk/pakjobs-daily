Q: What is the difference between government and semi-government jobs in Pakistan?

In simple words, both are in the public sector, but they are run and governed differently.

Government jobs are posts in federal or provincial ministries, divisions, departments and attached offices – for example the Federal Secretariat, provincial secretariats, police, district administration, government schools and hospitals, and the courts' ministerial staff. Salaries follow the Basic Pay Scale (BPS-1 to BPS-22), and rules for service, promotion, leave and pension come from the civil servants' laws and rules of the federation or the province. Hiring for many of these posts goes through the public service commissions (FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, KPPSC, BPSC) or through departmental committees with a testing service.

Semi-government jobs are in organisations that are owned or controlled by the government but run with their own board and their own service rules. Typical examples are autonomous and statutory bodies, regulatory authorities, state-owned corporations and companies, utilities and some public sector universities. Practical differences you usually notice:

- Pay structure: many use their own pay scales or special pay packages instead of plain BPS, so salary can be higher or lower than a comparable BPS post.
- Service rules: promotions, pension or provident fund, and transfer rules are set by the organisation's own regulations, so they vary from one body to another.
- Recruitment: they usually advertise directly and often use a testing service (NTS, PTS, OTS etc.) instead of a public service commission.
- Contract jobs: semi-government bodies more often hire on contract or project basis, so always check whether a post is permanent, contract or project-based before you apply.
- Job security: regular government posts are generally more secure; in semi-government bodies it depends on the organisation and the type of appointment.

Tip: the advertisement normally tells you which category the post is in. Read the terms (permanent/contract, pay scale, pension or not) carefully and confirm on the organisation's official website.
