Q: How do I get jobs in ASF Pakistan?
URL: https://www.quora.com/unanswered/How-do-I-get-jobs-in-ASF-Pakistan

The Airports Security Force (ASF) is a federal force that protects Pakistan's airports, so it hires from all provinces according to the quota written in each advertisement. Here is how to approach it step by step:

1. Watch for the official advertisement. ASF jobs are published in national newspapers and on official government job portals. Each ad lists the posts (entry-level uniformed posts, ASI/SI, female screening staff, and civilian posts like clerks, computer operators and drivers), the quota by province, the age limit and the education needed. Some officer posts are filled through FPSC, so keep an eye on FPSC ads too.

2. Check eligibility carefully. You normally need a valid CNIC, the right domicile for the quota, the required education (matric/intermediate for entry posts, a bachelor degree for ASI/SI), and the age and physical standards (height, chest) for uniformed posts. Exact numbers change between ads, so read the one you are applying for.

3. Apply exactly as the ad says, with correct CNIC, domicile and education details. Small mistakes are a common reason for rejection.

4. Prepare for the selection steps. Usually there is a physical test (measurement and running), a written MCQ test (general knowledge, Pakistan studies, Islamiat, English, Urdu, basic maths), an interview, and then medical and character verification. Start running daily a few weeks before the test and practise MCQs every day.

5. Keep your documents ready: CNIC, domicile, educational certificates, photos and attested copies.

6. Beware of fake ads. ASF never asks for money on WhatsApp or through an agent to "confirm" a seat. If someone asks for payment to guarantee selection, it is a scam.
