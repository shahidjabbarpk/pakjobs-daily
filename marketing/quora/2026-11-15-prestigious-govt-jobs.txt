Q: What are some prestigious government jobs in Pakistan?

"Prestige" in Pakistan usually comes from three things: authority, career growth and job security. Based on that, these are the government careers most people consider top-tier:

1. Central Superior Services (CSS) – Officers selected through the FPSC's CSS exam join occupational groups such as the Pakistan Administrative Service (PAS), Police Service of Pakistan (PSP), Foreign Service, Inland Revenue, Customs, Audit & Accounts and others. These officers can rise to the highest posts in the federal and provincial governments, which is why CSS is seen as the most prestigious civil exam.

2. Provincial Management Service (PMS) – The provincial equivalent, through PPSC, SPSC, KPPSC or BPSC. PMS officers serve in provincial administration, e.g. as assistant commissioners and in provincial departments.

3. Commissioned officers in the armed forces – Pakistan Army, Navy and Air Force commissions (through ISSB) carry great respect, structured careers and good facilities.

4. Judiciary – Civil judges and higher judicial posts, selected through the relevant high court or public service commission exams, are highly respected and need a law degree and legal practice requirements.

5. Specialist professional posts – doctors and specialists in government hospitals, university faculty (lecturers, assistant professors and above), engineers in major public departments, and officers in institutions like the State Bank of Pakistan or regulators.

6. Senior posts through FPSC general recruitment – officer-grade posts (BPS-16/17 and above) in federal departments, FIA, Intelligence Bureau and others.

Which one is "best" depends on your degree, age and interest. Most of these have strict age limits and eligibility, so check the official rules (FPSC/provincial commission websites, ISSB, etc.) early and plan your preparation.
