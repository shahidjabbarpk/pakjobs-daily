Q: How do I join agencies like ISI, FIA, IB, and NCTA in Pakistan? I couldn't join the army due to keloids on my chest.

Not getting into the army does not close the door to security and law-enforcement work. The civilian agencies recruit through their own processes, and each one has its own medical standards, so it is worth checking each advertisement carefully.

FIA (Federal Investigation Agency): this is the most open route. FIA hires constables, ASIs, sub-inspectors, inspectors, cyber crime staff and office staff. Many officer-level posts (Inspector, Assistant Director) are advertised through FPSC, while other posts are advertised in national newspapers and on official government job portals. Uniformed posts have a physical test (height, chest, running), a written test and an interview, followed by a medical. If you have a degree in computer science or IT, the cyber crime wing is a strong option.

IB (Intelligence Bureau): it advertises posts from time to time in newspapers and on official portals, often with a testing body named in the ad. Read the eligibility and medical criteria in that ad.

Other civilian intelligence and counter-terrorism bodies mostly recruit officers through FPSC/CSS or through their own official advertisements. Be very careful here: these names are often misused in fake ads and WhatsApp messages. A real advertisement is printed in national newspapers or on an official .gov.pk site, and no one can sell you a seat.

About the medical issue: a skin condition that disqualified you in one service may or may not matter in another, because standards differ. The honest answer is to read the medical requirements in each ad and, if needed, get a specialist's report before applying. Don't let one rejection stop you.

Practical tips: keep running and exercising, read a newspaper daily for current affairs, solve past papers, and keep your documents (CNIC, domicile, degrees) attested and ready.
