Question: How do I get a job in a bank in Pakistan?

Banks in Pakistan hire all year, but their recruitment works a little differently from normal government jobs, so it helps to know where and how to look.

1. Know the types of banks that hire
- State Bank of Pakistan (the central bank): officer posts through its own ads and a testing body named in the ad. Very competitive.
- Public sector banks: advertise in newspapers and on their official careers pages.
- Private and Islamic banks: run large programmes such as Management Trainee Officer (MTO), Cash Officer and Branch Banking Officer, mostly through their own careers portals.
- Microfinance banks: hire a lot of branch and field staff, including in smaller cities.

2. Pick the right entry point
Most fresh graduates enter as Cashier/Teller, OG-III / Branch Banking Officer, or through a trainee programme. Specialised degrees (IT, finance, accounting, law) open doors to IT, audit, risk and compliance roles. Each bank sets its own degree, grade/CGPA and age requirements, so read every ad carefully.

3. Prepare for the written test
Almost every bank test has:
- Quantitative: percentages, ratios, profit and loss, simple and compound interest.
- English: grammar, vocabulary and reading comprehension (often the whole test is in English).
- Analytical reasoning and general knowledge.
- Basic banking awareness: account types, cheques, loans, markup, KYC, and what the State Bank does.
Practise timed tests; speed matters as much as accuracy.

4. Interview
Have a short, clear introduction ready, know the bank's basic products, and be able to explain simply why you want a banking career. Following economic news (inflation, policy rate, exchange rate) helps a lot.

5. Avoid fake "bank bharti" ads
A real bank never asks you to send money to a personal mobile wallet for a job. Apply only via the bank's official website or the testing service named in the official ad.
