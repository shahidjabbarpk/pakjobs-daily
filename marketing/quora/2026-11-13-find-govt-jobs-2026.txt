Most government jobs in Pakistan are now advertised online, so the trick is knowing the official sources and checking them regularly. Here is what works:

1. Official recruitment portals (check these first)
- National Jobs Portal (NJP) – most federal ministries and departments post vacancies here and you apply with your CNIC.
- FPSC (fpsc.gov.pk) – federal BPS-16 and above posts and CSS.
- Provincial commissions: PPSC (Punjab), SPSC (Sindh), KPPSC (KP), BPSC (Balochistan), AJKPSC.
- NTS, OTS, PTS and similar testing services – many departments hire through them.
- Armed forces: joinpakarmy.gov.pk, joinpaknavy.gov.pk, joinpaf.gov.pk.

2. Newspapers
Jang, Express, Dawn and Nawa-i-Waqt still carry many department adverts, especially on Sundays. Always note the advertisement number.

3. Match the job to your eligibility before applying
Check qualification, age limit (and any age relaxation), domicile/quota and the last date. A lot of applications get rejected only because of domicile or age.

4. Keep your documents ready
CNIC, domicile, attested degrees, photos and a short CV. Deadlines are often only 2–3 weeks after the advert.

5. Avoid scams
A real government job never asks you to pay someone to "confirm" the job. Verify every advert on the official website.
