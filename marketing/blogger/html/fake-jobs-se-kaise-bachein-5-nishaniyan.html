Har saal hazaron log naukri ke naam par dhoka khate hain. Ye 5 nishaniyan yaad rakhein:

  1. Koi "job confirm" karne ke liye paise maange.
  2. Ishtihar mein department ka naam ya advertisement number na ho.
  3. Sirf WhatsApp number diya ho.
  4. Baghair test/interview ke naukri ka wada.
  5. Sarkari idare ke naam se gmail/yahoo email.

Hamesha job ko official .gov.pk website ya National Jobs Portal par confirm karein. Poori guide: fake job scams.
