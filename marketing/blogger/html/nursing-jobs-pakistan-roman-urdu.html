Pakistan mein nurses ki demand hamesha rehti hai – sarkari hospitals, private hospitals, clinics aur bahar ke mulkon mein bhi. Agar aap nursing parh rahe hain to ye baatein zaroor jaan lein.

Qualifications

  • General Nursing diploma – recognised nursing school se.
  • BSN – 4 saala degree, behtar posts aur overseas chances.
  • Midwifery / Public Health – extra diploma jo kai posts mein kaam aate hain.

Registration

Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council (PNC) mein registration zaroori samjhein. Zyada tar sarkari aur achhe private idaray valid registration mangte hain, aur bahar jaane ke liye bhi chahiye.

Jobs kahan hoti hain

  • Sarkari hospitals – health department aur public service commission ke ads.
  • Military aur autonomous hospitals.
  • Private hospitals/clinics – seedha CV ke sath apply karein.
  • Overseas – sirf legal channel se.

Test aur interview

Anatomy, fundamentals of nursing, medical-surgical nursing, pharmacology aur infection control ke MCQs ki practice karein. Degree, registration, experience letter aur CNIC ki file tayyar rakhein.

Salary aur age limit har idare ke ad mein alag hoti hai, official website se confirm karein. Poori guide: Nursing Jobs in Pakistan Guide.
