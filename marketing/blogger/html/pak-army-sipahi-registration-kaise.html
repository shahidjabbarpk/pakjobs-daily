Pak Army mein sipahi (soldier) bharti hona bohat se naujawanon ka khwab hota hai. Process mein kai marhale hote hain, is liye pehle se tayyari zaroori hai. Yahan mukhtasar rehnumai hai.

Aam sharait

  • Pakistani shehri, valid CNIC aur domicile.
  • Ad mein di gayi umar ki had ke andar.
  • Trade ke mutabiq taleem (aksar Matric ya us se zyada).
  • Qad, seena, wazan aur nazar ke mutaliqa standards.
  • Saaf background, koi criminal record nahi.

Marhale

  1. Registration (recruitment centre ya official portal).
  2. Physical test – daud, push-ups wagaira.
  3. Medical.
  4. Likhit test – English, Urdu, math, general knowledge.
  5. Interview.
  6. Final merit list.

Tayyari ke tips

Roz thori daud karein aur dheere dheere barhayein, Matric level math aur English revise karein, aur apne asal documents aur photocopies tayyar rakhein.

Khabardar: army bharti mein koi agent ya paise ka lein-dein nahi hota. Jo shakhs seat ke badle paise maange, us se door rahein. Tareekhein aur sharait hamesha official army website par confirm karein.
