FIA (Federal Investigation Agency) Pakistan ki federal law-enforcement agency hai – immigration, cyber crime, human smuggling aur corruption jaise cases par kaam karti hai. Har province mein iske offices hain, is liye FIA waqtan-fawaqtan bharti karti rehti hai. Federal job, izzat aur promotion ka raasta – isi liye FIA jobs ki bohat demand hai.

FIA mein kaun si posts aati hain?

  • Constable / Head Constable – uniform wali field posts, physical test + written test.
  • ASI aur Sub-Inspector – investigation posts, aam tor par bachelor degree.
  • Inspector / Assistant Director – officer posts, inmein se kayi FPSC ke zariye aati hain.
  • Cyber crime / technical posts – CS, IT, telecom walon ke liye.
  • Office staff – LDC, UDC, steno, computer operator, driver, naib qasid.

Selection ke marahil

  1. Official website/portal par online apply (jo ad mein likha ho).
  2. Uniform posts ke liye height/chest aur running test.
  3. Written test – GK, Pak Studies, Islamiat, English, Urdu, current affairs, basic maths.
  4. Interview, phir medical aur character verification.

Tayari kaise karein?

Uniform post ke liye abhi se rozana daurna shuru karein – physical mein bohat log fail hote hain. Rozana akhbar parhein, past papers aur mock tests hal karein, documents attested rakhein. Age limit, domicile aur quota har ad mein alag hota hai – hamesha official FIA ya FPSC website par confirm karein.

Fake FIA ads se bachein

FIA ka naam istemal kar ke WhatsApp par jaali ads chalte hain. Asli ad national akhbar aur sarkari website par aata hai. Jo "pakki seat" ke paise maange – wo fraud hai.
