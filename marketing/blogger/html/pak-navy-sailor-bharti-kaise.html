Pak Navy mein sailor ban kar mulk ki khidmat karna bohat se naujawanon ka khwab hai. Bharti ka tareeqa har ad mein thora alag hota hai, isliye hamesha official Navy website aur ad ko final samjhein. Ye general rahnumai hai.

Bunyadi shartein

  • Pakistani citizen, valid CNIC aur domicile.
  • Ad mein di gayi umar ki hadd.
  • Trade ke mutabiq matric ya intermediate.
  • Qad, wazan, seena aur nazar ke mayaar.
  • Saaf background, koi criminal record nahi.

Marhale

  1. Registration – official channel se.
  2. Physical test – running aur exercises.
  3. Written test – English, maths, general knowledge, Pakistan Studies.
  4. Medical.
  5. Interview.

Tayari kaise karein

Roz subah running aur halki exercise shuru karein, class 9-10 ki maths aur English grammar dohrayein, aur current affairs ke MCQs hal karein. Original aur photocopy documents ek file mein rakhein.

Fake agent se bachein

Armed forces ki bharti mein kisi agent ya rishwat ki zaroorat nahi. Jo paisa maange us se door rahein aur sirf official website ya recruitment office se apply karein.
