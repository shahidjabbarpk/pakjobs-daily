Bohat se umeedwar test pass kar lete hain lekin verification par ek kaghaz kam hone ki wajah se moqa kho dete hain. Sarkari mehkame documents kai dafa check karte hain – apply karte waqt, test wale din, interview par aur aakhri verification mein. Is liye behtar hai ke ek tayyar file pehle se bana lein.

Ye documents taqreeban har job mein chahiye

  • CNIC – valid honi chahiye. Abhi expiry date check kar lein, renewal mein waqt lagta hai.
  • Domicile certificate – provincial aur district quota ke liye.
  • Degree/certificate aur DMC – Matric, Inter, Bachelor ya diploma, dono cheezein.
  • Taaza passport size tasveer – digital copy bhi rakhein kyun ke online form par upload karni parti hai.
  • Experience letter – agar post experience mangti hai, official letterhead par.

Kuch jobs mein ye bhi

HEC/IBCC equivalence, HEC attestation, character certificate, disability ya minority quota ka certificate, sarkari mulazim ke liye NOC, aur age relaxation ka saboot.

File kaise tayyar karein

  1. Har document ek dafa saaf colour mein scan karein aur phone + email mein save kar lein.
  2. 2–3 sets attested photocopies banwa lein (gazetted officer se – ad mein jo likha ho wo dekhein).
  3. CNIC, certificates aur form par naam ki spelling aur date of birth bilkul ek jaisi honi chahiye.
  4. Fee challan aur online form ka print sambhal kar rakhein.
  5. Interview par originals zaroor sath le kar jayein.

Yaad rakhein: kisi agent ko paise de kar documents "banwane" ki koshish na karein – official kaghaz sirf mutaliqa daftar hi deta hai. Har mehkame ki requirements alag hoti hain, is liye poora ad parhein aur official website par confirm karein.
