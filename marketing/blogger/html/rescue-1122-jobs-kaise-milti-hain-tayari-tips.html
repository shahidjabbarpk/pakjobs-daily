Rescue 1122 wo emergency service hai jo accident, aag, imarat girne, sailab aur medical emergency mein sab se pehle pohanchti hai. Bohat se naujawan is mein naukri chahte hain kyun ke kaam izzat wala bhi hai aur logon ki madad ka mauqa bhi. Yahan short mein samjhein ke bharti aam tor par kaise hoti hai.

Kaunsi posts aati hain?

  • Rescuer / Fire Rescuer: front-line team – aag bujhana, rescue, pehli madad. Aam tor par Matric ya Inter + fitness.
  • Emergency Medical Technician (EMT): ambulance mein pehli medical care.
  • Driver: sahi category ka driving licence aur tajurba.
  • Control room, computer operator, office staff aur upar ki officer posts.

Selection ke aam steps

  1. Online apply (CNIC, domicile, degrees, photos tayar rakhein).
  2. Physical test – khaas tor par running.
  3. Written test – GK, English, Urdu, maths, everyday science, first aid.
  4. Interview, medical aur verification.
  5. Selection ke baad academy mein training.

Tayari kaise karein?

  • Test se kam az kam 2 mahine pehle hafte mein 4–5 din running shuru karein.
  • Basic first aid seekhein: CPR, khoon rokna, jalna, haddi tootna.
  • Rozana MCQs aur timed mock test lagayein.
  • Interview mein batayein ke aap emergency mein calm kaise rehte hain.

Fake ads se bachein

Rescue 1122 kabhi kisi "agent" ko paise dene ya zati JazzCash/Easypaisa number par fee bhejne ko nahi kehti. "Paise do, job pakki" = scam. Height/age/fitness ki shartein aur dates sirf official ad ya website se confirm karein.
