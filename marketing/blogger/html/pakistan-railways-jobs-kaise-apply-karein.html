Pakistan Railways mulk ke sab se bare idaron mein se hai – Karachi se Peshawar tak stations, workshops aur offices. Is liye yahan Matric se le kar engineer tak har level ki posts aati rehti hain. Lekin isi wajah se "railway bharti" ke naam par fake messages bhi bohat chalte hain.

Kis qisam ki posts aati hain?

  • Field / operations: stations, trains, signal aur track ke kaam.
  • Workshop / technical: DAE, trade certificate ya trade test wali posts.
  • Office: clerk, computer operator, assistant.
  • Professional: engineer, doctor, nurse, accountant, IT aur railway schools ke teachers.
  • Officer level: ye aam tor par FPSC / CSS ke zariye hoti hain, seedha railway ad se nahi.

Asli ad kahan milta hai?

Asli ads akhbaron, Pakistan Railways ki official website aur aksar National Jobs Portal par aate hain. Ad mein likha hota hai ke test kaunsi testing body le gi aur apply kahan karna hai. Bharti aksar division-wise hoti hai (jaise Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi), is liye quota aur domicile zaroor check karein.

Apply karne ka tareeqa

  1. Pura ad parhein: post, BPS, division, quota, last date.
  2. Documents tayar rakhein: CNIC, domicile, degrees, photos.
  3. Sirf official link ya pate par apply karein; fee sirf official tareeqe (bank challan waghera) se – kabhi kisi bande ko nahi.
  4. Application ID aur fee receipt sambhal kar rakhein.
  5. Test ki tayari: GK, Pakistan Studies, English, Urdu, basic maths aur post se mutaliq sawalat.

Fake bharti ki nishaniyan

"Paise do, job pakki" – ye sab se bara red flag hai. Ad mein akhbar ka hawala ya testing body na ho, ya fee JazzCash/Easypaisa ke zati number par mangi jaye, to door rahein.

Rules aur test details badalti rehti hain, is liye hamesha official website ya ad se confirm karein.
