Airports Security Force (ASF) Pakistan ke airports, musafiron aur jahazon ki hifazat karti hai. Ye ek federal force hai, is liye har sooba ke umeedwar apne quota ke mutabiq apply kar sakte hain. Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta aur Multan jaise bare shehron mein posting, uniform wali izzat aur regular promotion ki wajah se ASF ki jobs bohat pasand ki jati hain.

ASF mein aam taur par kaun si posts aati hain?

  • Corporal / Lance Corporal type entry posts – aksar Matric ya Intermediate wale.
  • ASI aur Sub-Inspector – zyada tar Bachelor degree mangti hain.
  • Officer posts – kuch FPSC ke zariye aati hain, is liye FPSC ke ads bhi dekhte rahein.
  • Female staff – passenger screening ke liye khawateen ki bharti bhi hoti hai.
  • Civil staff – clerk, computer operator, driver, cook waghera.

Selection ke mumkin marahil

  1. Online ya form ke zariye application (CNIC, domicile aur taleem ki sahi details).
  2. Qad, seena aur daud ka physical test (uniform posts ke liye).
  3. Written test – General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, English, Urdu aur basic math ke MCQs.
  4. Interview, phir medical aur character verification.

Tayari ke 3 asaan tips

Test se kuch hafte pehle roz daud shuru karein, roz thore MCQs hal karein, aur apne documents ki file pehle se tayyar rakhein. Har ad ki age limit, qad aur quota alag ho sakte hain – is liye official ad zaroor parhein aur wahin confirm karein.

Khabardar: ASF kabhi WhatsApp par ya kisi agent ke zariye "seat confirm" karne ke paise nahi mangti. Aisa koi kahe to wo fraud hai.
