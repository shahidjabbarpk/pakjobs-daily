Har saal hazaron Pakistani rozgar ke liye bahar jaate hain – khaas kar Gulf mumalik. Lekin bahar ki naukri ke naam par sab se zyada log apni jama-punji bhi yahin ganwate hain. Agar aap official system samajh lein to safe raasta chun sakte hain.

Teen official idaray / raaste

  • Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment: sarkari idara jo bahar rozgar ka nizam chalata hai, recruiting agents ko licence deta hai aur Protector of Emigrants offices chalata hai.
  • OEC (Overseas Employment Corporation): sarkari recruiting company – foreign employers aur government-to-government programs ki jobs apni official website par deti hai.
  • Licensed agents (OEPs): private promoters jin ke paas Bureau ka valid licence ho. Licence number Bureau ki website par check ho sakta hai.

Safe tareeqa – qadam ba qadam

  1. Jobs OEC ki website, licensed agent ya mashhoor foreign employer se dhoondein.
  2. Koi bhi paisa dene se pehle agent ka licence Bureau ki website par check karein aur unke registered office jayein.
  3. Job offer / contract likhit mein lein: employer, post, tankhwah, duty hours, rehaish.
  4. Passport, CNIC, degrees aur skill certificates (jaise NAVTTC/TEVTA courses) tayar rakhein.
  5. Medical sirf us mulk ke approved centre se karwayein.
  6. Jahan lagu ho, safar se pehle Protector stamp lagwayein – ye aap ko legal overseas worker register karta hai.

Fake agent ki nishaniyan

  • Licence number nahi, ya Bureau ki list se match nahi karta.
  • Bina interview ke bohat zyada tankhwah ka wada.
  • Jaldi cash ya zati account mein paise, receipt nahi.
  • Kaam ke liye visit visa – aksar mumalik mein ye ghair-qanooni hai aur giraftari / deport ho sakta hai.
  • Passport bina receipt ke agent apne paas rakh le.

Dhoka ho jaye to Bureau of Emigration ya Protector office mein shikayat darj karwayein. Fees aur rules badalte rehte hain – official OEC aur Bureau websites se confirm karein.
