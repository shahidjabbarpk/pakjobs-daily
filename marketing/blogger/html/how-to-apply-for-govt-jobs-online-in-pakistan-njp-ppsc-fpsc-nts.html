Aaj kal Pakistan mein zyada tar sarkari naukriyon ke liye online apply hota hai. Har idare ka tareeqa thora mukhtalif hai, lekin basic steps aik jaise hain:

  1. Ishtihar ghaur se parhein: post, seats, taleem, age limit, domicile aur last date.
  2. Account banayein: NJP, PPSC, FPSC ya NTS ki official website par CNIC ke sath.
  3. Profile mukammal karein: naam bilkul CNIC jaisa, taleem marks ke sath, tasveer.
  4. Fee sirf official challan / online tareeqe se jama karein.
  5. Last date se 2–3 din pehle submit karein – aakhri din website slow hoti hai.
