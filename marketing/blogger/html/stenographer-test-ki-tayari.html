Stenographer aur steno-typist ki posts wafaqi aur sooba'i mehkamon, adaalton aur daftaron mein aati rehti hain. Test ki tafseel har mehkame ki alag hoti hai, isliye ad ghaur se parhein aur official site se confirm karein.

Test mein kya hota hai

  • Written test: English grammar, comprehension, general knowledge, kabhi basic computer.
  • Shorthand test: tay speed par imla likhwaya jata hai.
  • Transcription/typing: imla ko time limit mein type karna, accuracy ke sath.
  • Interview.

Shorthand speed kaise barhayein

  • Achhi kitab ya ustaad se shorthand system theek se seekhein.
  • Pehle slow dictation, phir speed barhayein. Parhne ke qabil na ho to speed bekar hai.
  • Roz 20-30 minute practice, hafte mein ek lambi nishast se behtar hai.
  • Apne likhe hue notes khud parh kar dekhein.

Typing aur zubaan

Bina keys dekhe typing ki practice karein, punctuation aur spelling par tawajjoh dein, aur roz angrezi akhbar parhein.
