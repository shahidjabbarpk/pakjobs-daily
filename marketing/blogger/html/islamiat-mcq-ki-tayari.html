Islamiat NTS, PPSC, FPSC aur bohat se recruitment tests ka hissa hota hai. Sawal aksar seedhe hote hain, is liye munazzam tayari se is section mein achhe number mil sakte hain.

Kin topics ki tayari karein

  • Bunyadi aqaid aur arkaan: Islam ke arkaan aur imaan ki bunyadi baatein.
  • Quran: nuzool, jam o tadween aur mashhoor suraton ke mazameen.
  • Hadith aur Sunnat: Hadith ki ahmiyat, aqsaam aur mashhoor majmoay.
  • Seerat: Nabi (PBUH) ki zindagi ke aham waqiaat tarteeb se.
  • Khulafa-e-Rashideen aur Islami tareekh.
  • Islami muashrati iqdaar.

Aasan plan

  • Pehla hafta: ek mustanad kitab se short notes banayein.
  • Doosra hafta: topic-wise MCQs hal karein, ghalat jawab notes mein likhein.
  • Teesra hafta: timer laga kar mock test dein aur ghaltiyan dohrayein.
