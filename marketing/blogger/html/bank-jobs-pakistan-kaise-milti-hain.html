Graduate naujawanon ke liye bank ki naukri Pakistan mein sab se pasandida career mein se ek hai. Banks saal bhar hiring karte hain, training achi dete hain aur promotion ka rasta saaf hota hai. Masla ye hai ke bank ki bharti aam sarkari naukri se thori mukhtalif hoti hai, is liye bohat log sahi jagah dekhte hi nahi. Yahan short mein samjhein.

Kaun kaun se bank hire karte hain?

  • State Bank of Pakistan: central bank – officer posts, competition bohat zyada.
  • Public sector banks: sarkari ya adh-sarkari banks – ads akhbar aur unki official careers website par.
  • Private aur Islamic banks: Management Trainee, Cash Officer, Branch Banking Officer programmes – zyada tar apne careers portal par.
  • Microfinance banks: chhote shehron aur districts mein bhi field aur branch staff.

Aam posts

Cashier/Teller, OG-III ya Branch Banking Officer, Management Trainee Officer (MTO), IT/audit/risk officers, aur support staff (driver, guard, messenger).

Selection ke aam steps

  1. Online apply – CV aur documents tayar rakhein.
  2. Written test: English, maths (percentage, ratio, profit-loss, interest), reasoning, GK aur basic banking.
  3. Interview ya assessment day.
  4. Verification aur training ke baad branch posting.

Tayari ke tips

  • Rozana maths ke sawal practice karein – har bank test mein aate hain.
  • English grammar aur reading behtar karein; aksar test sirf English mein hota hai.
  • Banking terms seekhein: current/savings account, cheque, loan, markup, KYC.
  • Inflation, policy rate aur dollar rate ki khabar asaan alfaaz mein samjhein.

Fake "bank bharti" se bachein

Asli bank kabhi zati JazzCash/Easypaisa number par paise bhejne ko nahi kehta. Sirf bank ki official website ya ad mein likhi testing service ke zariye apply karein. Eligibility, age aur degree ki shartein har bank ki alag hain – official ad se confirm karein.
