Written test pass karne ke baad interview final merit ka faisla karta hai. Kuch aham baatein:

  • Saare original documents aur attested copies file mein rakhein.
  • Saaf, simple formal kapre pehnein.
  • "Apne baare mein batayein" aur "ye job kyun chahiye" ke jawab pehle se soch lein.
  • Current affairs aur apne subject ki basic baatein revise karein.
  • Jo sawal na aaye, adab se bata dein – andaza na lagayein.
