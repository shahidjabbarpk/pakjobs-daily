Junior Clerk, LDC, UDC aur Assistant ki posts Pakistan mein sab se zyada advertise hone wali sarkari naukriyon mein se hain. Har department, district office, court aur university ko clerks chahiye hote hain. Education aksar Matric ya Inter hoti hai, is liye competition bohat sakht hota hai – achi tayari hi farq dalti hai.

Selection kaise hoti hai?

  1. Written test (MCQs) – testing service ya public service commission leti hai.
  2. Typing test – bohat si clerk posts ke liye English (kabhi Urdu) typing speed chahiye. Kitni speed – ye ad mein likha hota hai.
  3. Interview aur documents ki verification.

Test mein kya aata hai?

  • General knowledge aur Pakistan Studies
  • Islamiat (ya non-Muslim ke liye Ethics)
  • English grammar, synonyms/antonyms, application likhna
  • Urdu grammar aur muhavare
  • Basic maths: percentage, average, ratio
  • Computer basics: MS Word, Excel, shortcuts, email

6 hafte ka asaan plan

  • Hafta 1–2: Pak Studies, Islamiat, GK – rozana 1–2 ghante, short notes banayein.
  • Hafta 3–4: English aur Urdu grammar + rozana 20 maths sawal.
  • Hafta 5–6: computer basics, past papers aur time laga kar mock tests.
  • Rozana: 20–30 minute typing practice – speed aahista aahista banti hai.

Zaroori baatein

Ad do dafa parhein – education, age, domicile aur testing body har post ki alag hoti hai. Documents pehle se attested rakhein. Jo "pakki clerk job" ke paise maange, wo fraud hai – selection sirf test aur merit par hoti hai.
