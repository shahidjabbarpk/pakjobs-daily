CSS (Central Superior Services) wo competitive exam hai jis se Federal Government ke BPS-17 officers chune jaate hain. Ye exam FPSC leta hai. Har saal hazaron graduates bethte hain aur thore hi log final allocation tak pohanchte hain – lekin selection sirf aapke apne marks par hoti hai, sifarish par nahi.

CSS se kaun si services milti hain?

Pakistan Administrative Service, Police Service, Foreign Service, Inland Revenue, Customs, Audit & Accounts, Information Group aur kayi aur groups. Group aapki merit position, apni preference aur quota rules se tay hota hai.

Exam ke marahil

  1. Screening test (MPT) – MCQs ka test, pass karne par hi written exam de sakte hain.
  2. Written exam – compulsory papers (English essay, precis, general science & ability, current affairs, Pakistan affairs, Islamic studies) + optional subjects.
  3. Medical, psychological assessment aur aakhir mein viva.

Eligibility

Recognised university se bachelor degree, Pakistani shehriyat, age limit ke andar (kuch categories ko relaxation) aur attempts ki had. Ye rules FPSC update karta rehta hai – social media ke bajaye hamesha official FPSC website par confirm karein.

Shuruat kaise karein?

  • Pehle official syllabus aur past papers download karein.
  • English par rozana kaam – zyada log essay aur precis mein fail hote hain. Har hafte ek essay likh kar check karwayein.
  • Rozana akhbar, current affairs ke short notes.
  • Optional subjects woh lein jo samajh aate hon, sirf "scoring" sun kar nahi.
  • Saal ya zyada ka waqt rakhein – aur sath sath doosri sarkari jobs mein bhi apply karte rahein.
