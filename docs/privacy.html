PakJobs Daily – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

PakJobs Daily ("the app") helps people in Pakistan find government and private jobs. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. The app is independent and is not affiliated with any government entity; job information is collected from public official websites, which remain the authoritative source.

Information you give us

  • Profile (optional): your name and WhatsApp/phone number, only if you post a job or a comment. They are shown publicly on your posts and comments so applicants can contact you.
  • Job posts and comments you choose to publish, and reports you file about abusive or fraudulent content.
  • CV Maker data is stored only on your device and turned into a PDF on your device. We never upload it.

Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous account ID: Google Firebase creates an anonymous identifier so you can manage your own posts. It is not linked to your name, email or phone unless you add a profile.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • Preferences (language, district, saved jobs, alert categories) are stored on your device.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, SMS or call logs.

How we use information

  • To show job listings, results and roll-no notices, and to send job alerts and deadline reminders you turn on.
  • To publish private job posts after moderation, and to remove scams, spam and abusive content.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is processed by service providers that run the app: Google Firebase (database and anonymous sign-in) and Google AdMob (ads). Public posts and comments are visible to all users.

Retention and deletion

Job posts expire automatically after 30 days. You can delete your own posts and comments at any time in the app. To delete your profile or any other data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for job seekers aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by Firebase security rules that let users change only their own content.
