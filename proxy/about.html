About PakJobs Daily

PakJobs Daily helps job seekers in Pakistan find genuine opportunities quickly. On this website you can search the latest private-sector and government vacancies from across the web by job title and city, for free and without signing up.

The Android app

The PakJobs Daily app (coming soon on Google Play) adds:

  • Government job alerts from the National Jobs Portal, PPSC, FPSC, KPPSC, SPSC, BPSC, NTS and the armed forces, checked every hour
  • Private jobs from employers and the web, filtered by city and industry
  • Results, test schedules, interview calls and roll number slips
  • Timed mock tests, MCQ practice and official past papers
  • A free CV maker with PDF download
  • Full English and Urdu support

Where the jobs come from

Web job listings are provided by Careerjet. Government notices come from the official websites of the commissions and departments, which remain the authoritative source. Every job links back to the original advert, so you always apply at the source. PakJobs Daily never asks job seekers for any fee.
