Delete your PakJobs Daily account

This page explains how to delete the account you created in the PakJobs Daily Android app (developer: PakJobs Daily, contact shahidjabbarpk@gmail.com) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open the PakJobs Daily app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

If the app asks you to log in again first, tap Logout, log in with the same Google or email account and repeat the steps. This is a Firebase security check for older sessions.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email shahidjabbarpk@gmail.com from the email address you used to log in, with the subject "Delete my PakJobs Daily account". If you logged in with Google, use that Gmail address. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login (Google or email account link) and account ID
  • Your profile: name, WhatsApp number and city
  • All private job posts you published
  • All comments you wrote

What is kept

  • Reports you filed about scam or abusive content are kept without your name, so moderators can act on them.
  • If an account was banned for fraud, the ban record (an ID only, no personal details) is kept to stop the same account posting scams again.
  • Saved jobs, settings and CVs are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
