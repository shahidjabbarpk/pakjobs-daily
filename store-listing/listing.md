## Full description (max 4000)

```
PakJobs Daily brings new government and private jobs in Pakistan to your phone, every day, in English and Urdu.

Stop checking ten websites. PakJobs Daily watches the official job portals and recruitment commissions for you, and alerts you when a new vacancy for your district is announced.

🏛️ GOVERNMENT JOBS
• New vacancies from the National Jobs Portal, PPSC, FPSC, KPPSC, SPSC, BPSC, NTS, Pak Army, Pak Navy, PAF and other departments
• Checked every hour, with the last date, BPS scale, qualification and number of seats
• Filter by province, district, category, qualification and commission
• "Last date soon" list so you never miss a deadline

💼 PRIVATE JOBS
• Jobs posted by employers, reviewed before they go live
• Thousands more private-sector jobs from across the web (jobs by Careerjet)
• Filter by city and industry: sales, IT, office, teaching, driver, factory, health and more

🔔 ALERTS & REMINDERS
• Notifications for new jobs in your province and district
• Reminders before the last date of jobs you saved

🏆 RESULTS, ROLL NO SLIPS & SCHEDULES
• Test schedules, results, interview calls and roll number slips from the commissions in one place

📝 EXAM PREPARATION
• Timed mock tests in the pattern of PPSC, FPSC, KPPSC, SPSC, BPSC, NTS, armed forces and police tests
• Subject-wise MCQ practice with shuffled questions
• Official past papers you can read inside the app
• Step-by-step guide on how to attempt the test
• Downloadable practice papers with answer key

📄 FREE CV MAKER
• Make a clean, professional CV with photo, education, experience, certificates and awards
• Download it as a PDF in minutes; your CV stays on your phone

🌐 ENGLISH & URDU
• Full app in English and اردو

IMPORTANT
PakJobs Daily is an independent app and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by or representing any government entity. Job information is collected from the public websites of the departments and commissions, such as njp.gov.pk, ppsc.gop.pk, fpsc.gov.pk, kppsc.gov.pk, spsc.gov.pk, bpsc.gob.pk, nts.org.pk, joinpakarmy.gov.pk, joinpaknavy.gov.pk and joinpaf.gov.pk. Every job links to its original advertisement; always confirm details and apply at the official source. PakJobs Daily never asks job seekers for any fee. Never pay anyone to get a job.

Website: https://pakjobs-daily.vercel.app
Privacy policy: https://shahidjabbarpk.github.io/pakjobs-daily/privacy.html
Contact: shahidjabbarpk@gmail.com
```
